## How it works

When you buy something, you agree on a price. If it gets cheaper afterwards, your price stays the same. Even if it's a day later.

But there's a smart way out. Did you buy **online**? Then you have at least **14 days to change your mind** from the day your parcel arrives. During that time you can send it back without a reason. And then order it again at the lower price.

Do the maths first. If returning costs €5 and the price difference is €4, you've gained nothing. See until when it can go back in the [return checker](/en/return-checker/).

## Just ask first

Returns cost the shop money too. So it's smart to email or chat first: "I see it's cheaper now. Can you give me the difference? Otherwise I'll send it back." A shop doesn't have to say yes. But often that's easier for them than a return.

## The tricky part

After Black Friday come Cyber Monday, the December deals and the January sales. Prices go up and down. Once you've bought something, you stop watching. And by the time you notice, your cooling-off period is often over.
