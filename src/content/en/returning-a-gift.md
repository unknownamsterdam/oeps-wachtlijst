## How it works

A gift bought **online** falls under the buyer's cooling-off period. That's at least **14 days**, and the clock starts when the parcel reaches the buyer. Many shops give more time, like 30 days. Check the [return periods per shop](/en/returns/).

Did a gift arrive on 2 December, from a shop with 30 days? Then the last day is around **1 January**. While you're still eating oliebollen.

Was the gift bought in a **regular shop**? Then there's no cooling-off period. Exchanging is only possible if the shop offers it, and almost always with the receipt.

## The tricky part

The buyer and the receiver are two people. The receiver notices it doesn't fit. But only the buyer can send it back, through their own account. And they forgot the date long ago.
