## What is going on?

On **1 October 2026 KPN** and **Vodafone** raise the price of mobile plans by **3.3 percent**. That is the inflation of 2025, as the Dutch statistics office (CBS) calculates it.

It applies to plans taken out or renewed **before 1 July 2026**. At KPN that is about **€0.83** extra on a plan of €25 per month and **€0.99** on €30 per month. Extra bundles are not included.

## Can you cancel for free?

Usually **not**. The inflation correction was already in the general terms you got when you signed up. According to Kassa, that is why it is no valid reason to cancel for free during the contract.

Does your contract end? Then you can switch.

## What can you do?

- **Check when your contract ends.** That is your moment to compare.
- **Check your bill.** Does the amount match what was announced?
- **Want to know what your terms said?** Find your contract or the terms.

## And other subscriptions?

If the price increase is not in your terms, it works differently. Read our explanation about [price increases of subscriptions](/en/explained/price-increase/).
