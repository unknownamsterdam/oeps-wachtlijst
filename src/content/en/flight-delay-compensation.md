## When are you owed money?

Do you arrive **more than 3 hours late** at your final destination? Then you often have a right to compensation. The amount depends on the distance, per person:

- up to 1,500 km: **€250**
- 1,500 to 3,500 km: **€400**
- over 3,500 km: **€300** for 3 to 4 hours late, **€600** for more than 4 hours

You do not have to accept a voucher. You can ask for the money.

## When not?

With **extraordinary circumstances** you get no compensation. Think of bad weather, an air traffic control strike or a bird strike. The airline must prove that this was the case.

According to the European Court, a technical problem with the plane is not extraordinary. Then you usually are owed money.

## Waiting at the airport

- **From 2 hours** of delay: food and drink from the airline, and a hotel if needed.
- **More than 5 hours** of delay: you may skip the trip and ask for your ticket money back.
- Does the airline not do this? Keep your receipts. You can claim the costs later.

## And if your flight is cancelled?

Then you choose: your money back or another flight. Compensation can also apply. Check the Consumentenbond page for your situation.

## What is happening with the European rules?

There was a proposal to give compensation only after **5 hours**. In June 2026 that was dropped: the **3-hour limit stays** and the amounts stay the same. The European Parliament still has to approve it, and it is not known when it starts. Until then the rules above apply.

New in the agreement: for a delay of more than 3 hours the airline must email you within 4 days about your rights, and within 30 days either pay or explain why not. Automatic payouts are not included.
