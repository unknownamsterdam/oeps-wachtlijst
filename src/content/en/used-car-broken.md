## What is a legal warranty?

The car must be good for **normal use**. That is called the legal warranty. It is not the same as a warranty certificate, or a package a garage sells you.

With a used car you may expect **a bit less** than with a new one. The ANWB names: the advert or what the seller said, the age and the mileage, and the price.

## Who has to prove what is wrong?

- **First 12 months:** if the car breaks down, the law assumes the problem was already there at purchase. The **dealer** has to prove it wasn't.
- **After that:** **you** have to show that the problem was already there at purchase.

An example the Consumentenbond gives: if the car stops (safely) driving within a year, that is a reason to hold the dealer to account.

## What can you ask for?

1. **A free repair or replacement.** You choose. It may not cost you anything.
2. If that doesn't work, or is unreasonable, you can sometimes **cancel the purchase** (your money back, the car back).
3. Or you ask for a **lower price**.

## What if the dealer won't help?

- Report the problem **in writing** and give a deadline to fix it. The Dutch government recommends this too.
- Is the dealer a member of the **Geschillencommissie** (many garages, via Bovag)? Then you can file your complaint there. Radar says this costs only a small amount.
- **Don't sign** anything in which you give up your legal warranty. In 2022 Radar saw with a hidden camera that one of the ten dealers visited asked for that. Only one in ten told them unprompted that they have a right to the legal warranty. That is about this test, not about all dealers.

## From a private person?

Then it is **not a consumer purchase**. You have a heavier duty to check the car before you buy. And the seller can't be a member of the Geschillencommissie. So in a dispute you more often have to go to court.

## Want less hassle afterwards?

- Take a **test drive**, preferably with someone who knows cars.
- Consider a **pre-purchase inspection**.
- Ask about maintenance, damage and history, and get the answers **in writing**. What was agreed, you have to be able to prove.
- Check the number plate with the RDW.
