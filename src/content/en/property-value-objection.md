## Why do you care about the WOZ value?

The WOZ value is the value your municipality gives your home. Tax depends on it. A higher value means you pay more, says the Consumentenbond.

The value on your 2027 letter is about **1 January 2026**. That is called the valuation date. A price change after that date doesn't show yet.

The Waarderingskamer expects an average rise of **5.5 to 7.5 percent** for homes (NL Times). That is an average. Your municipality sets the value of your home itself.

## How to object

1. **Look at the date** on the WOZ decision. From that date you have **6 weeks**.
2. **Check the value.** Is what it says about your home right? For example the size. Also compare with homes in your area.
3. **Call or email your municipality first.** That sometimes solves it already, says the Rijksoverheid.
4. **Object** with your municipality. The decision and your municipality's website say how.
5. **Wait for the decision.** The municipality must decide in the calendar year in which you objected.

Disagree with the outcome? Then you can appeal to the court.

## What does it cost?

Nothing. Many people hire an agency, but you don't have to: objecting costs nothing.

## Why OEPS!?

The WOZ letter comes once a year and the time is short. If it lands in your digital message box, it's easy to miss. If you put the letter in OEPS!, OEPS! remembers the last day to object. So you don't have to think of it yourself.
