## Why is it getting more expensive?

According to the Radar survey (November 2025), premiums have risen a lot in three years. Where some pay about 15 euros a month, a growing group pays more than 50. That share rose from 10 to 20 percent.

## Things that are often not reimbursed

- Neutering or spaying
- Preventive care
- Conditions your pet already had before the insurance started

More than 80 percent of respondents had a claim that was not fully reimbursed. 52 percent thought the insurance was not worth it. This is one survey, not a yardstick for every policy.

## How to compare

1. **What do you pay now?** Per month and per year.
2. **What is excluded?** Read the terms on existing conditions, neutering and prevention.
3. **When can you cancel?** The notice period is in your policy terms.
4. **What happens at the new insurer?** Often a waiting period of about 30 days for illness, and existing conditions are usually not covered.
5. **How old is your pet?** Many insurers don't accept older animals, often from 6 to 8 years.
6. **Cancel only once the new one is running.** That way you are never without cover.

According to Overstappen.nl, a new contract taken out online can be withdrawn within 14 days. Check that in the terms.
