## Hand luggage now

Right now **your airline decides** what you may take for free and what costs extra. Budget airlines often charge extra for a small wheeled suitcase. So check what is in your fare when booking.

## What changes in 2027?

The European Parliament voted for new rules on **7 July 2026**. They are expected to start in **2027**. Tickets you book before then still fall under the old rules.

- A **small personal item** may come along for free, such as a handbag or small backpack.
- Airlines must show the **price including hand luggage** from the start of booking.
- A **cheaper fare without hand luggage** remains possible.
- A **cabin suitcase stays** paid, according to Radar. That demand was dropped.
- No extra costs anymore for correcting a typo in your name.
- You get a right to a digital boarding pass without an extra app.

Sources differ on the exact size and weight. So always check with your own airline.

## Arrange luggage when booking

Luggage is best arranged at booking:

- **Check what is in your fare.** Hand luggage, checked luggage or nothing.
- **Is your luggage worth a lot?** Declare that before departure, for example when booking. Then you can agree on a higher compensation. That costs extra.
- **Prices differ per airline.** So add luggage before you pay, so you are not surprised at the gate.

## Your suitcase is lost, delayed or broken

1. **Report it right away** at the baggage desk at the airport and fill in a **PIR form**. Keep the copy and the file number.
2. **Take photos** of your suitcase and the damage.
3. **Buy only what you really need**, such as clothes and toiletries, and keep the receipts.
4. **Claim in time:** damaged within **7 days**, delayed or lost within **21 days**. Report it in writing.

The airline is responsible for checked luggage. The maximum is about **€1,860 to €1,890** per person. Travel insurance can cover on top of that. Check your terms.

## Tips for on the road

- Photograph your suitcase and its contents before the trip.
- Take valuable or fragile things in your hand luggage.
- Keep your luggage tag and boarding pass until you have everything back.
