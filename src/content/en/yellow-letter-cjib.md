## What is the yellow letter?

The CJIB sends about **eight million** traffic fines a year. About 85 out of 100 are paid on time.

For the rest, there used to be an increase straight away. Since **1 July 2026** a **free reminder** comes first. That is the yellow letter. The bottom half is yellow, so you don't overlook it.

Note: according to Autoreview this is **a trial** for now.

## How it works

1. You get a traffic fine.
2. Don't pay **within eight weeks**? Then the yellow letter comes.
3. You have **two weeks** to pay the normal amount. Nothing is added.
4. Still not paid? Then the increase follows: **one and a half times** the fine, and later **three times** the fine.

An example: a € 100 fine becomes € 150 and then € 300.

## Can't pay in one go?

Don't wait. Ask for a payment plan **yourself**. You do that on **cjib.nl** (look for "pay in instalments"). The CJIB doesn't do it automatically.

According to the CJIB leaflet you can pay fines of € 75 or more in instalments. If the answer is yes, the letter says exactly how to pay. Check cjib.nl for the current rules.

## Watch out for fake letters

The yellow letter is easy to recognise, so fake ones are too. Don't scan a QR code without checking. Better log in on cjib.nl with DigiD and see whether your fine is really there.

## Why OEPS!?

Nobody forgets a fine on purpose. The letter sits among the mail and after a few weeks you can't remember. A fine is just a bill with a date. Put it in OEPS!, and OEPS! remembers the payment date and sends you a nudge before it's too late.
