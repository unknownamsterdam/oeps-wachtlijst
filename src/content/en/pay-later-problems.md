## What goes wrong?

Pay later is easy, but the bill keeps going if you miss it. This often goes wrong:

- **You miss the reminder.** With Riverty, customers say they got no free reminder and straight away a letter with collection costs. Riverty says the reminder emails can end up in your **spam folder**.
- **Your order does not arrive.** With Klarna the bill keeps going if you cannot return an order. This happens mostly with rogue webshops.

## What is not allowed?

Since **31 October 2023** a code of conduct applies to pay-later services:

- They must communicate **clearly** about what you still have to pay.
- You must get an **extra, free reminder** before costs are added.

## What can you do?

1. **Check your spam folder** and your email history for reminders.
2. **Contact** Klarna or Riverty and ask how the costs were built up.
3. **Keep everything** if your order does not arrive: the order confirmation and messages with the webshop.
4. **Do you have a debt?** Your municipality can help. You can also ask for a payment delay.

## Why an arrear can weigh heavily

According to Van Bruggen (October 2026) a negative **BKR registration** can stay visible for five years, even if the debt has been paid off. That can make a mortgage harder. So pay on time and take reminders seriously.
