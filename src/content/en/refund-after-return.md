## How it works

Sending something back within the cooling-off period? Then the online shop has **14 days** to refund you. The clock starts when you let them know you don't want to keep it.

The shop may wait a bit. Until the parcel is back, **or** until you show that you sent it. Whichever comes first.

You get the price back, plus the standard delivery costs you paid. The same way you paid.

Sounds simple. Yet in a large study by the Dutch Consumers' Association, more than **1 in 3 online shops** got it wrong at least once: too late, or not at all.

## Money not in? Here's what to do

1. **Find your proof.** The receipt or email from the pick-up point, with the tracking code. That shows you sent it back in time.
2. **Email the shop.** Say you sent the product back on that date, and that the money must be back within 14 days. Give them a few days.
3. **Still nothing?** Report it to ConsuWijzer, or to the disputes committee if the shop is a member. Paid by credit card or PayPal? Ask them if they can help too.
