## How it works

With pay later there are two parties. The **shop**, where you send the parcel. And the **payment service**, like Klarna or Riverty, that sends you the bill.

They don't automatically know what the other is doing. The shop only sees your parcel once it has arrived and been processed. Until then, the payment service thinks you simply have to pay.

So when you return something, you do two things:

1. Send it back following the shop's steps. Keep your proof of postage.
2. Report the return to your payment service. With Klarna, your payment is then paused.

## The tricky part

Around Black Friday you often have several orders at once. Each with its own payment date and its own last return day. One forgotten report, and you get a reminder for something that went back ages ago. Or you pay twice and have to chase your money.
