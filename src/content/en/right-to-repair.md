## What has changed?

Since **31 July 2026** European rules on repair apply. That means you do not always have to throw away a broken device. You can ask the manufacturer to fix it.

This is about devices that can be repaired, such as:

- washing machines
- vacuum cleaners
- mobile phones
- tablets

## What can you ask for?

- **A repair.** The repair must be free or at a reasonable price.
- **Within a reasonable time.** You should not have to wait for months.
- **Spare parts.** Manufacturers must offer spare parts at a reasonable price.
- **Information.** The manufacturer must clearly tell you where to go for a repair.

## Repair or replacement?

Does your device break within the warranty and do you choose a **repair** instead of a replacement? Then your legal warranty becomes **at least 12 months longer**. A replacement does not give you that extra year.

## After the warranty

Also after the warranty, repairs become easier. What you pay yourself depends on the product. The price must still be reasonable.

## Why OEPS!?

Warranties often run out quietly. OEPS! keeps an eye on the warranty of your device. So you know in time that you can still ask for a repair.

## Note: the Dutch law is not finished yet

The European rules already apply. The Netherlands is still working out the rules. The bill 'Bevordering reparatie' is still in the Dutch House of Representatives. So in practice it can still differ.

This is general information, not legal advice. If in doubt, ask the manufacturer or the Consumentenbond.
