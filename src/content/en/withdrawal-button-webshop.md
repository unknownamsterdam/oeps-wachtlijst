## What has changed?

Since **19 June 2026** every webshop and app must have a **withdrawal button**. With that button you cancel your online purchase, without emailing or calling.

## How does it work?

- The button is **clear and easy to find**. The webshop chooses how it looks, for example with the text "cancel my purchase".
- You can use it for the **whole 14-day cooling-off period**.
- You **do not need an account**. Logging in is allowed, but not required.
- You fill in only what is needed, and get a **confirmation right away**.

## What does not change?

Your cooling-off period stays **14 days**. The button only makes it easier to use. It applies to what you buy **online**, not in a shop.

## Cannot find the button?

Then the webshop is not following the rules. That has consequences for the webshop:

- Your cooling-off period can be extended automatically **up to a year**.
- The ACM can give **fines**.

You can report it to the ACM. Keep a screenshot of the order page and your confirmation email.

## Why OEPS!?

The button only helps if you are in time. OEPS! remembers until when you can cancel or return. So you use the button in time.

This is general information, not legal advice. If in doubt, ask the ACM or the Consumentenbond.
