## How it works

Buying online is different from buying in a shop. You haven't seen the product yet, so you get a **14-day cooling-off period**. The clock starts when your parcel arrives.

Not everything can go back. Something made especially for you, food, or make-up with a broken seal is excluded.

## Sounds simple, right?

It is. Until you have three parcels from three web shops, with three delivery dates. Who still has an overview then?
