## How it works

Black Friday is just buying online, with a discount. Your rights stay the same. You have **14 days to change your mind**, and the clock starts when your parcel **arrives**. After that you have another 14 days to actually send it back.

Many shops give you more time themselves, for example 30 days. That differs per shop. Check the [return periods per shop](/en/returns/).

Not everything can go back. Something made especially for you, or sealed personal care you've opened, doesn't count. Discount or not.

## The tricky part

On Black Friday you don't order one thing. You order five, from different shops. Each parcel arrives on a different day. So each parcel has a **different last return day**. Almost nobody keeps track of that.
