## When do you get money back?

Do you arrive more than **30 minutes** later than planned? Then you can claim part of your travel costs from NS. On an **Intercity Direct** that's already possible after **15 minutes**.

You get between **half and the full amount** of your ticket, depending on how long the delay lasted. Amounts under **€ 2.30** are not paid out.

## How to claim

1. **Keep your ticket** until the claim is done.
2. Fill in NS's **questionnaire**.
3. Start the claim via **Mijn NS**. No account? Then use the form.
4. You get a **confirmation by email**. It usually takes a few days.

Do it **usually within one month**. After **three months** or more, nothing is paid out.

You need a valid ticket: an e-ticket, a single-use chip card, or checking in and out with your OV-chipkaart, NS Business Card or bank card.

## When do you get nothing?

- If you knew in advance that the trip would take longer.
- If another operator caused the delay.
- In cases of force majeure, such as a national power cut.

## And during a strike?

NS **scrapped the travel cost allowance during strikes** (maximum € 25 per day) **on 4 September 2026**. During a strike you can still claim money back through **Geld Terug bij Vertraging**, in the same way as above. So NS looks at how long your trip took, not at the cost of a taxi or another trip.

## Why OEPS!?

Claiming money back is only possible for a short time. After that it's gone. OEPS! has no link with NS. OEPS! does remember other things with a short deadline: what can still go back to the shop, when your warranty ends and when a bill must be paid.

This is general information, not advice. For your own trip, check the NS website.
