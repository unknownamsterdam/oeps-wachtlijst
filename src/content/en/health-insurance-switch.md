## How it works

Dutch health insurance runs per year. You can switch **once a year**, around New Year. If you do nothing, your insurance simply continues, at the **new premium**.

Want to switch? There are two ways:

1. You take out a new insurance **before 31 December**. Your new insurer cancels your old one for you.
2. You cancel yourself **by 31 December**. Then you have **until 31 January** to choose a new one.

## The tricky part

The premium changes every year, and sometimes so does what's covered. That's in one email in November. And December is busy enough already.
