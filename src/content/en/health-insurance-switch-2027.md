## What's new?

The health insurance premiums for 2027 are out. The average premium for basic insurance is **€ 173.13 per month**. That is **€ 14.83 more** than this year.

The **compulsory deductible** (eigen risico) goes from € 385 to **€ 400**.

Every insurer sets its own premium. What is best for you depends on your own situation. OEPS! does not tell you which one to choose.

## Until when can you switch?

You can switch **once a year**, around New Year.

1. Take out a new insurance **before 31 December 2026**. Your new insurer cancels your old one for you.
2. Or cancel yourself by **31 December 2026 at the latest**. Then you have **until 31 January 2027** to take out a new one. It starts on 1 January.

Do nothing? Then your insurance continues, at the new premium.

Want it step by step? See [Cancelling your health insurance. Until when?](/en/explained/health-insurance-switch/).

## What do you look at?

- The **premium** per month.
- The **deductible**.
- Does your **supplementary insurance** still suit you?

## Why OEPS!?

It's one email in November, and December is busy enough. OEPS! keeps track of your fixed costs, including your insurance. So you don't miss the date.
