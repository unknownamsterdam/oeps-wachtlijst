## How it works

A subscription often starts with a **fixed period**, for example one year. During that time you're tied to it. After that it **keeps running by itself**, without an end date. From then on you can almost always cancel, with a notice period of **1 month at most**.

Newspapers, magazines and some contracts have different rules.

## The tricky part

Every subscription has a different start date, a different period and a different notice period. Gym, streaming, newspaper, phone. Who knows all of that by heart?
