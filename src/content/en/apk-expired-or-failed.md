## What happens if your APK has expired?

Driving without an APK is **a punishable offence**. The police can fine you. And after **two months** the RDW (the Dutch vehicle authority) checks whether your car has been tested. If not, a fine can follow.

Have an accident with an expired APK? Then your insurer can decide that you weren't insured at that moment.

## May you still go to the garage?

Yes, but only:

- within **two months** of the expiry date, and
- if you can show an **appointment**.

## Your car failed

- Did your car fail **before** the expiry date? Then you may keep driving until the expiry date, at your own risk. The police can still fine you, for example for faulty lights.
- Did your car fail **after** the expiry date? Then you have **two months** from that date to repair it and have it tested again. You may only drive to the garage.

## Avoiding a fine

Is your car standing still and you don't want to have it tested? Then you can **suspend** your number plate. The APK obligation then stops. A car that is simply parked is not automatically exempt.

## November is busy

At the end of 2026 the APK of many cars expires at the same time. According to Autorai it concerns about **529,000 passenger cars** in November. So book in time. You may have your car tested up to two months before the expiry date, without your expiry date changing.

## Why OEPS!?

An APK is easy to forget. OEPS! gets the date from the RDW when you enter your number plate. Six weeks ahead you get a nudge.

This is general information, not legal advice. For your situation you can contact the RDW or your garage.
