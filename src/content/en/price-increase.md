## How it works

A company can't just raise the price. It has to be **in the terms beforehand**, and it has to tell you **in time**. For phone, internet and TV you can often **cancel for free** when the price goes up. But only within a short time after the message.

## The tricky part

Your right to cancel depends on one email. Miss it, or react too late? Then you simply pay the new amount. And that happens with more and more subscriptions.
