## How it works

The cooling-off period is **14 days** after the day your parcel arrived. Many shops give more, like 30 days.

The good news: within that time you only have to **let them know** you don't want to keep it. An email or the shop's return form is enough. After that you have another **14 days** to actually drop off the parcel.

Did you let them know in time, but the parcel left a day later? Then you're not too late.

## Really too late?

Then the shop no longer has to take it back. But it doesn't hurt to ask:

1. **Ask nicely.** Many shops still say yes for a day or two, especially if the product is new.
2. **Exchange instead of a refund.** Sometimes an exchange or a voucher is still possible.
3. **No luck?** Sell it. Unused and in its box, it sells quickly on Vinted or Marktplaats.
