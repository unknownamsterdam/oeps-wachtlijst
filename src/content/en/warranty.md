## How it works

There are two kinds of warranty. The **manufacturer's warranty** is an extra, often 2 years. The **legal warranty** you always have, and it can last much longer. For an expensive TV or fridge, sometimes 6 to 10 years.

Does something break in the **first year**? Then the shop has to prove that you broke it. After that it gets harder.

## The tricky part

Having the right is one thing. Showing it is another. For that you need the receipt and the purchase date, often years later.
