## How to tackle it

1. **Look around.** At the neighbours, in the shed, in the letterbox, at a pick-up point. Check for a card or a message in the courier's app.
2. **Email the web shop.** Include your order number, the tracking code, and that you received nothing. Ask for a solution: a new parcel or your money back.
3. **Keep everything.** Emails, screenshots of the tracking, the date you reported it.
4. **Set a final deadline.** Nothing happens? Then write that you're giving a last date, after which you want your money back.

## The tricky part

In December you get lots of parcels at once, from different couriers, each with its own app. Then you don't quickly notice that one is missing. You only find out when the present isn't there on the day.
